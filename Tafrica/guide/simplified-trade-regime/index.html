The Simplified Trade Regime (STR)

The Simplified Trade Regime is an AfCFTA framework for small-scale and informal cross-border traders. Nigeria was selected as the West African pilot country in 2026.

What the STR Changes

Before STRWith STR
Multiple agency forms requiredSingle simplified digital declaration
Manual, in-person paperworkDigital submission from your phone
Full commercial invoice requiredSimplified declaration for low-value goods
Complex documentation for all shipmentsStreamlined process for small shipments
No special protection for women tradersExplicit protections for women and MSME traders

Current Status

Nigeria’s Customs Service is finalizing the Standard Operating Procedure for the STR. The system is not fully live yet, but Nigeria will be among the first West African countries to implement it as the pilot country. We’ll update this page and the community as rollout continues.
