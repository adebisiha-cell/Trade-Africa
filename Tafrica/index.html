The Problem

Trade policy has changed. Most traders have not heard about it.

Intra-African trade makes up only 18% of the continent’s total trade, well below Europe’s 69% and Asia’s. The issue is not a lack of interest from African businesses. Most traders have not had clear, practical guidance on how to trade formally.
