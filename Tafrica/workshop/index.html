Trade Africa MSME Trade Readiness Workshop

A half-day Lagos event for Nigerian SME traders moving from informal trade to AfCFTA-ready, finance-eligible businesses.

What attendees leave with

  • A clear view of AfCFTA rights and the required documentation
  • First steps toward a verified trade identity
  • An introduction to on-chain financial-history infrastructure for African MSMEs
  • Connections to verified buyers and suppliers

Who it’s for

Importers and exporters operating within West Africa — fashion/textile traders and agro-processors alike.

Status

We’re planning for Q4 2026. Attendance will be free; join the community below for registration updates.

Delivered with trade finance and capacity-building partners.
