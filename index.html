01 · The Problem

Trade policy has moved. Most traders haven’t heard.

Intra-African trade sits at just 18% of the continent’s total trade, far below Europe’s 69% and Asia’s 59%, not because African businesses don’t want to trade with each other, but because most traders have never had access to clear, practical guidance on how to do it formally.
